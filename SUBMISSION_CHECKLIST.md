## Category

Primary submission category: **Small Business Services**.

ShiftSecure helps essential-service employers respond to urgent vacancies without replacing their scheduling system. Job continuity is an important secondary impact, but the submission should not claim two primary categories.

## Claims policy

Do not claim real customers, revenue, transportation bookings, payments, compliance certification, or worker outcomes without evidence. Demo workers, expenses, outreach, rides, and arrival events remain clearly labeled simulated data.
