1. Open the landing page: “Shift filling is incomplete until the qualified replacement arrives.”
2. Enter the demo and introduce Harbor View’s uncovered 7:00 AM CNA shift.
3. Show four workers: Maria passes; unavailable, expired-credential, and late candidates are explicitly excluded.
4. Show the mobility comparison. The $31 option is late; the $36 option arrives at 6:42 AM.
5. Explain that policy—not an LLM—requires approval because $36 exceeds $30.
6. Run successful rescue. Point out the restricted $40 **mock** Prava authorization and **mock** Uber booking.
7. Follow departure, ETA, confirmed arrival, $36 receipt reconciliation, and audit trail.
8. Reset and inject payment rejection: booking never occurs.
9. Inject ride cancellation: duplicate spending is prevented and the rescue returns to discovery.
10. Close: ShiftSecure integrates with an employer’s existing scheduler and brings human-controlled agentic commerce to workforce continuity.
