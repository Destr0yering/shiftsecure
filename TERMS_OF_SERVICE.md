# ShiftSecure Terms of Service (Pilot Template)

**Status:** Draft for pilot review — not legal advice. Have counsel adapt this document to the organization, jurisdiction, pricing, and service model before accepting customers.

## 1. Service

ShiftSecure is a workforce-continuity coordination tool. It helps authorized organization users evaluate an uncovered shift, coordinate outreach, compare transportation options, route approvals, and record operational events. It does not employ workers, operate vehicles, provide medical care, guarantee staffing, or guarantee transportation availability or arrival.

## 2. Customer responsibilities

The customer is responsible for lawful scheduling and employment decisions, worker notices and consent, qualification verification, data accuracy, approvals, transportation-provider accounts, payment obligations, and monitoring every consequential action. Customer users must keep credentials secure and may not share accounts or use the service for unlawful discrimination, retaliation, surveillance, or unsafe work assignments.

## 3. Human control and AI limitations

AI-generated summaries are advisory and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unavailable. Deterministic policy checks and authorized human decisions control eligibility, approvals, spending, bookings, and status. Customers must review recommendations and remain responsible for employment, transportation, and safety decisions.

## 4. Third-party services

Transportation, payment, messaging, scheduling, mapping, identity, and cloud services are third-party services. Their availability, pricing, terms, privacy practices, and results are outside ShiftSecure's control. A simulated or sandbox event is never a representation that a live ride, payment, or worker arrival occurred.

## 5. Fees and refunds

Pilot fees, usage fees, transportation charges, taxes, refunds, and cancellation charges must be stated in an order form. The customer remains responsible for charges it authorizes with a provider.

## 6. Confidentiality and security

Each party must protect non-public information received from the other party. ShiftSecure will use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to a pilot. No system is completely secure; production customers must complete their own security and compliance review.

## 7. Data protection

The customer remains the controller/business for worker and operational data unless the parties agree otherwise. The parties should execute a data-processing agreement before production use involving personal, location, health, financial, or other regulated data.

## 8. Disclaimer

To the maximum extent permitted by law, the service is provided “as is” and “as available.” ShiftSecure disclaims warranties of uninterrupted service, error-free results, merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and guaranteed staffing, transportation, payment, or arrival outcomes.

## 9. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, or lost-profit damages. Any aggregate direct-damage cap, exclusions, governing law, dispute process, insurance requirements, and carve-outs for confidentiality, intellectual property, fraud, willful misconduct, or non-waivable liability must be negotiated in the signed order form and reviewed by counsel.

## 10. Indemnification

Indemnification obligations, defense procedures, control of settlement, insurance, and exclusions must be negotiated for the pilot. In particular, counsel should address claims arising from customer instructions, employment decisions, worker data, transportation-provider use, unlawful discrimination, and customer-provided content. This template does not create an operative indemnity until incorporated into a signed agreement.

## 11. Suspension and termination

ShiftSecure may suspend access for security, misuse, non-payment, or legal risk. Either party may terminate a pilot under its order form. On termination, access ends and data is returned or deleted according to the agreed retention schedule, legal obligations, backups, and any active dispute.

## 12. Contact and acceptance

The production legal entity, notice address, governing law, effective date, and click-acceptance record must be completed before customer onboarding.
