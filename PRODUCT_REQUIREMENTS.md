# Product requirements

Outcome: a verified, qualified replacement arrives. The manager can inspect the vacancy, candidate eligibility, transportation tradeoff, policy result, approval, payment authorization, booking, ETA, arrival, receipt, and audit history. Protected attributes are excluded. The system never fabricates qualifications, availability, payments, or rides.

MVP acceptance: Maria is selected explainably; $36 triggers the $30 approval threshold; no booking precedes approval and authorization; duplicate operations are idempotent; a decline blocks booking; a cancelled ride returns to discovery; all simulated activity is labeled.
