# Legal and risk readiness

This project contains pilot templates, not legal advice or a representation of regulatory compliance. Before accepting real customer or worker data, obtain counsel review and complete:

- Final Terms of Service, Privacy Notice, Acceptable Use Policy, and order form.
- Data-processing agreement and subprocessors list.
- Worker notice/consent and location-sharing language.
- Insurance review, including cyber, technology errors and omissions, general liability, and transportation-related coverage.
- Employment-law, wage-and-hour, anti-discrimination, accessibility, and local transportation review.
- Incident response, deletion, retention, backup, and access-request procedures.
- Vendor review for Google Cloud, Gemini/Vertex AI, transportation, payment, messaging, and scheduling providers.
- Production controls preventing simulated events from being represented as real outcomes.

Never promise that ShiftSecure is HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI DSS, GDPR, or other certified/compliant without an independent assessment and a specific verified claim.
