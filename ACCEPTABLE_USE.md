# Acceptable Use Policy (Pilot Template)

ShiftSecure may not be used to:

- Make decisions using protected or sensitive characteristics.
- Create false qualifications, availability, payment, booking, or arrival records.
- Bypass approval, spending, audit, or access controls.
- Transport a person without required consent, safety information, or provider authorization.
- Collect unnecessary precise location, health, financial, or identity data.
- Harass, retaliate against, surveil, or discriminate against workers.
- Violate employment, transportation, privacy, consumer-protection, or accessibility laws.
- Probe, disrupt, reverse engineer, or overload the service.

Customers must report suspected misuse, security incidents, unauthorized access, or unsafe operational outcomes promptly. ShiftSecure may suspend activity that presents security, legal, fraud, or safety risk.
